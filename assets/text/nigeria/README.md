## A note on the brief's wording

The brief asked for "all living in occupied Nigeria". Nigeria is not occupied —
it is an independent state — so the first axis is living **in** Nigeria, under
conditions of state failure, insecurity and colonial legacy rather than under an
occupying power. The structure you asked for is preserved; only the word is
corrected.

**On invented examples:** you waived verification for this dataset, so
constructed examples are included freely. The `row_type` column always says
which is which — `example` is real and sourced, `scenario` is constructed. You
get the volume without losing the ability to tell them apart.

## What the brief asked for, and where it landed

- **"Unusually higher rate of random checks at US airport"** → `AIR-001`. The
  mechanics are documented — roughly **5% of arrivals referred at random**, many
  more for cause, phone searches lawful without a warrant under the border
  search exception, inspections from 30 minutes to many hours. `AIR-001-005`
  states plainly that **no per-nationality secondary-inspection rate is
  published by any government**, so the disparity rests on consistent traveller
  reporting rather than a statistic. Same treatment as the Palestine dataset.
- **Psychological effects of being seen as bad** → `REP` (4 issues) and `PSY-001`.
  Nigeria is the only nationality in these five datasets that carries **a scam as
  its global shorthand**. The key row is `REP-001-003`: in a study of 50
  convicted romance scammers, **46% posed as white American men**, 12% as
  military, 10% as white European men. The actual fraudsters are invisible as
  Nigerians; ordinary Nigerians using their real names absorb the entire
  suspicion. A stereotype about *honesty* is worse than one about competence,
  because success can be re-read as better deception (`PSY-001-001`).
- **The dating and relationship version** → `PSY-002`. The romance-scam
  association poisons the ordinary approach; being used as a novelty and
  discarded is recorded as its own harm; and visa suspicion contaminates genuine
  relationships, which must be evidenced to a government in writing.
- **"Efficiency, growth, back pain"** → `PSY-006` and the `SOM` category, and
  this is where Nigeria has unusually strong data. **`SOM-002` is the row that
  answers the brief most directly**: among rural Nigerian women, those who
  appraised their domestic responsibilities as very stressful had **OR 2.88 for
  low back pain and 4.58 for neck and shoulder pain**, with high-demand/low-
  control conditions at OR 2.58. `SOM-001`: Nigeria may carry one of the
  greatest low-back-pain burdens in the world — **40–85% one-year prevalence**
  against 14–51% elsewhere in Africa, rural 70–85% against urban 39%, farmers
  74.4%, drivers 73.5%.
- **Five layers** → layers 1–5, with 24 rows at layer 5.
- **Weird and rare examples** → see below.
- **Non-English sources** → German (`STA-002`: BAMF's high share of purely
  formal decisions on Nigerian claims, the Dublin loop to Italy, the queer
  claimant refused because enforcement is "disputed"). **yandex.com returns a
  CAPTCHA to automated access and could not be used** — recorded rather than
  quietly skipped.

## The weird and rare ones, as asked

- **`BOR-002` — 509 women refused for arriving alone.** Saudi Arabia, Hajj 2012.
  Nigeria suspended pilgrimage flights in response.
- **`BOR-001` — eighty hours at Sal Airport.** Five Nigerians with valid
  documents, return tickets, accommodation and funds were detained by Cabo Verde
  and deported to **Senegal**. Now before the ECOWAS Court.
- **`BOR-001` — watchlisted for a social media post.** Three Nigerians refused
  entry at OR Tambo in 2026; Nigeria's own diaspora commission said one had been
  flagged for old online remarks about the South African government.
- **`AIR-003` — refused *departure* from their own country.** In 2020 Nigeria
  Immigration stopped 58 London-bound Nigerian doctors at Lagos: the flight was
  chartered for 42, 58 presented, and only two held UK visas.
- **`DOC-002` — a country that does not print its own passports.** Despite owning
  a minting and printing company. A 2021 shortage traced to government debts to a
  foreign supplier who stopped shipping. The fee tripled to ₦100,000 in August
  2025 as an anti-corruption measure; immigration officers said publicly it
  would not stop the racket.
- **`DIG-001` — a downloadable file that blocks a country.** Publicly
  distributed `.htaccess` blocklists covering Nigerian ISPs nationally — not
  targeted at identified bad actors. Merchants in forums describe simply
  removing Nigeria from the checkout country list.
- **`AIR-002` — deported to Ghana, then bussed home.** Under a third-country
  arrangement for West African nationals removed from the US.
- **`TRF-001` — the Oba's revocation, ignored.** In March 2018 the Oba of Benin
  revoked all juju oaths binding trafficked women. The 2024 US TIP report
  records traffickers continuing to perform the ceremonies anyway.
- **`TRF-002` — eight women not allowed to board the rescue flight.** May 2020,
  Lebanon: 50 trafficked Nigerian women were repatriated; activists reported
  eight were blocked, their employers refusing permission and alleging theft.

## The best-grounded numbers

- **`VIS-001`** — Schengen refusal rate **45.9%** in 2024 (111,201 applied,
  50,376 refused), costing Nigerians over **€4.5m / ₦7.1bn** in non-refundable
  fees. The UK refused **1,344,595** Nigerian applications 2005–Q1 2026, a 33.1%
  cumulative rate against a 14.8% global average. US B1/B2 refusal **57%** in
  FY2025, up from 46.51%.
- **`VIS-002`** — US visas cut to **single entry, three months** on 8 July 2025;
  issuance suspended from 1 January 2026.
- **`MAT-001`** — MMR **512 per 100,000** nationally, **over 1,000** in the
  north; about **4 maternal deaths per hour**; only **43%** of births have a
  skilled attendant.
- **`MAT-002`** — Nigeria accounts for **40% of the world's obstetric fistula
  cases**; 13,000 new cases a year; at current repair rates the backlog would
  take **83 years** to clear.
- **`WOM-003`** — child marriage: **Jigawa 63%, Zamfara 63%, Sokoto 61%, Katsina
  61%**; poorest quintile 67% against richest 6%.
- **`SEC-002`** — Chibok, 14 April 2014: 276 girls taken, **at least 91 still
  unaccounted for**. Since then at least 17 mass school abductions and 2,416
  students; ₦5bn in ransoms paid in one 12-month window.
- **`POL-002`** — the Okuwobi judicial panel's 309-page report found at least 48
  protesters shot, injured or assaulted at Lekki and used the word **massacre**.
  The Information Minister said the "only massacre recorded was in the social
  media."
- **`ECO-001`** — petrol **₦219.03 → ₦655.48** in twelve months (+199.26%);
  inflation 22.41% → **34.19%**; commuting up nearly 300%.
- **`PWR-002`** — only **46.57%** of registered electricity customers metered;
  over two million overbilled in 2024, producing **₦105.05bn** in credit
  adjustments.
- **`HLT-002`** — 8,560 Nigerian doctors registered with the UK GMC 2021–2024,
  **39% of all international registrations**; ~57,000 nurses to the UK in five
  years; 85,000–90,000 registered doctors but only **55,000 practising in
  Nigeria**.
- **`WRK-003`** — Bertrand & Mullainathan: white-sounding names received **50%
  more callbacks**, and a better CV raised callbacks 30% for white names but far
  less for Black ones — so the usual advice does not close the gap.

## Honest limits

- **84 of 487 rows are constructed** (`row_type: scenario`). You authorised this
  explicitly. They are plausible and labelled, never dressed as sourced.
- **317 of 487 rows are `confidence: low`** — money and time bands are
  order-of-magnitude estimates unless a `source_url` is present. 124 are `high`.
- **Four rows record that the evidence does not exist**, rather than inventing
  it: `AIR-001-005` (no published per-nationality screening rate),
  `DIG-001-006` (the geoblocking harm is illustrated by forums and a personal
  essay, never measured), `REP-002-004` (no sourced evidence that Nigerian men
  are algorithmically blocked on dating platforms — widely said, not
  demonstrated), `EDU-003-004` (no evidence credential evaluators flagged
  2022–23 transcripts over ASUU strike gaps), and `SOM-004-001` (no study
  combines somatic scales, back pain and distress in a Nigerian worker sample).
- **`WRK-001` deliberately distrusts both sides.** Official unemployment is
  4.3% under the new ILO methodology and was 33.3% under the old; an advocacy
  report claims 80 million youth unemployed. The first understates, the second
  is implausible, and the row says so.
- **`MAT-002`'s fistula estimates span an order of magnitude** — 45,166 in a
  recent modelling study against UNICEF's 400,000–800,000. Both are recorded;
  the spread reflects genuine measurement difficulty.
- **`WOM-003` notes a distinction usually lost**: the failed vote to delete
  constitutional section 29(4)(b) was not a vote to legalise child marriage, and
  the clause's operative scope concerns renunciation of citizenship.
- **`WRK-003` carries its own counter-evidence** — Darolia et al. found no
  callback gap when pairing typically-white first names with typically-Black
  surnames, which is recorded alongside the Bertrand & Mullainathan finding.
- Geographic and source skew toward Lagos, Abuja and the north-west, and toward
  UK/US/Canada destinations, because that is where the data is collected.
- `PWR` (2 issues) is the thinnest category; layer 5 is the thinnest layer at 24
  rows.
